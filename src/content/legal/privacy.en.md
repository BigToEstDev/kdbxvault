KDBX Vault is an offline password manager. This policy covers the app (Android and desktop) and this website.

## The app

- The app runs entirely on your device. Your databases, passwords and key files are never sent to us or to anyone
  else: there are no servers and no accounts.
- No network: the Android app does not have the Internet permission, the desktop app does not connect to the
  Internet. A link (the website, the source code, issues) opens in your browser only when you choose it.
- No analytics, ads, crash reports or telemetry.
- Your database is a file you choose. The app reads and writes only the files you pick: the database, key files,
  attachments, copies for sync.
- On the device the app keeps its settings and the list of recently opened databases. With biometric unlock on
  Android, the database password is stored encrypted with a key of the Android Keystore that never leaves the
  device. App data is not included in cloud backups.

## The website

- A static site on GitHub Pages: no analytics, no cookies, no third-party resources.
- Your choice of language and theme is kept in your browser (local storage) and is never sent anywhere.
- GitHub, as the host, may log technical data of requests, such as IP addresses — see the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- Downloads come from GitHub Releases; we see only the total number of downloads.
